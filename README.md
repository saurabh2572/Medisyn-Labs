# 🧪 Medisyn Labs — Customer Insights & Medicine Recommendation Project

### A Data Science Case Study by **Medisyn Labs**

---

## 📘 Project Overview

Medisyn Labs, a leading pharmaceutical company, sells a wide range of medicines for common and specific conditions through online platforms. Customers leave reviews, ratings, and comments detailing their experiences with each medicine.  

While sales are steady, Medisyn Labs suspects that valuable insights are hidden within this feedback — insights that can enhance **product formulation, marketing strategies, and customer support**.

As the **Lead Data Scientist**, the goal is to analyze this feedback and build intelligent models that can uncover patterns, predict outcomes, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The project focuses on four key problems:

1. **Sentiment Classification**  
   → Classify customer reviews as **positive** or **negative** using NLP and machine learning.

2. **Effectiveness Prediction**  
   → Predict the **effectiveness rating** of a medicine (1–5 scale) using review text and metadata.

3. **Customer Cohort Identification**  
   → Identify **clusters (cohorts)** of customers with similar reviews and ratings to understand behavioral patterns.

4. **Medicine Recommendation System**  
   → Develop a **personalized recommendation model** suggesting medicines based on **user symptoms** and **past reviews**.

---
